Course Video Translation Pipeline

Every lesson travels this road from English master to translated export — the teacher's own voice, lips re-synced on the real footage, subtitles included. Machines do the work; humans hold the two gates that matter.

System verified end-to-end · July 10, 2026

The road: master in, export out

Amber = a check or a human gate. Nothing final gets rendered until a person has approved it.

1
Ingestautomatic

A row in the catalog becomes one job per language. The video is probed and, if needed, converted to a clean high-bitrate working copy.

2
Transcribeautomatic

Two independent speech engines transcribe the same lesson, so neither grades its own homework.

✓Gate 1 the two transcripts are compared; disagreements (names, numbers, scripture references) get resolved before anything else happens.
3
Translateautomatic

A pinned AI model translates with the course glossary and style guide, quotes scripture from the published version (RVR1960 · NR2006), and writes to fit the clock. New ambiguous terms are flagged for a human to decide — decisions grow the glossary permanently.

✓Gate 2 a different vendor's model blind-checks every line for lost or changed meaning.
4
Re-voiceautomatic

The teacher's cloned voice speaks the translation, chunk by chunk, with timing auto-fitted to the original delivery.

✓Gate 3 the generated audio is transcribed back and compared — garbled spots get re-voiced.
5
Masterautomatic

Every track is normalized to the same broadcast loudness (−16 LUFS, −1 dB true peak) so a whole course sits at one volume.

6
Package for reviewautomatic

A watchable preview (translated audio over the original picture), a click-to-play review page, and draft subtitles land in Dropbox.

TEAM REVIEW — Audio QC

A native reviewer opens the review page from Dropbox, listens, and approves — or flags lines with corrections. Corrections are applied automatically, only the affected chunks are re-voiced, and the lesson comes back for re-review. Nothing moves past this gate on its own.

7
Lip-syncafter approval only

One continuous pass re-syncs the teacher's lips on the real footage (top quality tier, locked). Never runs until the audio has been approved at the gate above.

8
Finishautomatic

Spec checks (length lock, color tags, loudness re-verified) and final subtitles generated.

TEAM REVIEW — Final QC

Team watches the export and gives the final word.

9
Deliverautomatic

The finished .mp4 + .srt land in the course's Dropbox delivery folder, named and sorted: deliver/<course>/<video>__<lang>.
